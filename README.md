# 🔎 BizLens

### See the signal. Trust the decision.

> **BizLens is an Enterprise AI Decision Intelligence Platform that transforms scattered business files into evidence-backed, verified decisions.**

Businesses keep critical information across Excel spreadsheets, CSV exports, financial reports, sales data, and PDFs. BizLens brings these sources together, analyzes them with AI and machine learning, answers business questions using RAG, generates forecasts and recommendations, and — most importantly — **verifies AI-generated claims against the underlying data before presenting them.**

---

## ✨ Why BizLens?

Traditional BI tools show dashboards.

AI copilots generate answers.

**BizLens asks a more important question:**

> **Can we actually trust the answer?**

Every important AI-generated business claim can pass through an independent verification layer that checks:

- 📊 Numerical calculations
- 📄 Source evidence
- 🔗 Cross-source consistency
- 📐 Business rules
- 🎯 Retrieval quality
- ⚠️ Conflicting information

Instead of treating every AI response as equally trustworthy, BizLens provides a **verification status and confidence score** for each insight.
